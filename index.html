Execution Manager

1a

A ROS 2 component lifecycle manager structured as a MAPE-K feedback loop. Its runtime monitor grew from passive health checks into active channel monitoring, then split into separate health, diagnostic and guardian nodes as the requirements matured.

Fault & alarm mechanism

1b

A mutex-guarded persistent fault registry keyed by node and error code, with lifecycle state derived from first-seen and last-seen timestamps, and three recovery models for terminal, transient and channel faults, plus a 1 Hz warning heartbeat for vehicle-facing consumers.

Recording & replay

1c

Subprocess lifecycle management for session recording (fork and execl, PID and atomic state tracking, a clean SIGINT and waitpid shutdown) with time-based splitting, topic filtering and timestamped configuration and calibration snapshots, so a recorded drive can be re-simulated exactly.

Mashinak

Live · MIT

An interactive map of what a car does for its driver, from SAE Level 0 to Level 5. A brochure gives you a flat wall of acronyms, ESP and EBA and HAC and TJA, and no way in. Mashinak turns that wall into something you can click through: pick a level, pick a feature, and see what it senses, where the decision is made, what it moves, and which other features it depends on. Every feature is drawn as senses → decided in → acts through, which is the part brochures hide. AEB does not brake. It asks the ESC unit to brake.

The Global view is pure engineering; EU, US and CN overlay the regulatory regime per level, with rule numbers, so a feature that is mandatory in one market and merely permitted in another says so. State lives in the URL, so #eu/L3/ALKS is a link you can send someone. The whole content set is typed data in src/data/, ids as string-literal unions, which means a typo in a dependency fails the build instead of quietly breaking the graph.

it exists because engineers join automotive projects every day without ever having been told what ESC actually is

ELF/DWARF signal extractor

Shipped

A C++ port of a Python DWARF parser that pulls signal metadata out of ECU binaries to generate A2L calibration files. Around day five the port stopped being a port: the reference implementation I was matching turned out to be incomplete itself, missing array expansions and resolving some types wrongly. DWARF is a graph of type relationships linked by DW_AT_type, not a flat list, so extracting it correctly means walking that graph rather than reading it.

C++libelf / libdwA2L / ASAP2XCP over CAN

Alfred & Kiyan, humanoid robots

Prototype

Alfred came first, a wooden-frame prototype built on almost no budget, and became my bachelor's thesis platform, graded 20/20.
