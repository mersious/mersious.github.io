On the bench right now

Three problems I work on daily, inside the SoC department of an automotive company, on a production L4 stack.

Execution Manager

1a

A ROS 2 component lifecycle manager structured as a MAPE-K feedback loop. Its runtime monitor grew from passive health checks into active channel monitoring, then split into separate health, diagnostic and guardian nodes as the requirements matured.

Fault & alarm mechanism

1b

A mutex-guarded persistent fault registry keyed by node and error code, with lifecycle state derived from first-seen and last-seen timestamps, and three recovery models for terminal, transient and channel faults — plus a 1 Hz warning heartbeat for vehicle-facing consumers.

Recording & replay

1c

Subprocess lifecycle management for session recording — fork/execl, PID and atomic state tracking, clean SIGINT shutdown — with time-based splitting, topic filtering and timestamped calibration snapshots, so a recorded drive can be re-simulated exactly.

Heart-rate and blood-oxygen measurement improved by 25% under sustained movement. Three hardware generations, each shaped by on-site feedback.
